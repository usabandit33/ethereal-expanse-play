# Ethereal Expanse v8.1

## Gameplay
- **Connectable ponds:** dig (**G**) within ~2.75 units of another hole to link basins; bridge water fills the gap.
- **Moat blocks Ground Drake:** filled water is impassable to the Ground Drake (strafes around or holds and breathes fire).
- **Ground Drake HP 56** (was 28) — longer fight, more value in moat control.
